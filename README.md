# Discovering Short-Term Fatigue-Management Patterns: Power Naps vs. Coffee

## Project Description
The project is a data mining study, and the problem type is **pattern discovery**, as opposed to prediction accuracy.
The problem is using a dataset available on the Kaggle platform to compare the effectiveness of power naps and coffee as a method to improve alertness.
The project will focus on using discovery-oriented data mining techniques such as clustering, association rule mining, anomaly detection, and dimensionality reduction to discover the reasons why certain results are achieved or not achieved based on the levels of sleep.

## Dataset
**Power Nap vs Coffee Effectiveness Dataset**
https://www.kaggle.com/datasets/prince7489/power-nap-vs-coffee-effectiveness-dataset

- Data format: CSV
- Data size: 500 rows x 11 columns
- Data description: Simulated fatigue management sessions where the levels of sleep and the type and duration of intervention are varied to measure the levels of alertness  before and after the intervention, as well as the levels of productivity and mood.

## Discovery Question
- What are the conditions for high levels of alertness and/or high levels of productivity/mood based on the levels of sleep, type and duration of intervention, and occupation?
- What are the conditions for the occurrence of side effects? Are there any anomalies in the responses based on similar conditions?

## Techniques to be used
- Clustering techniques such as K-means and Hierarchical clustering.
- Association rule mining techniques such as Apriori and FP-growth.
- Anomaly detection technique.
- Dimensionality reduction techniques such as PCA.
